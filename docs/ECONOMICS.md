# Economic policy

For each closed monthly period, Net Subscription Revenue equals collected subscription receipts minus transaction-level PayPal/payment fees, applicable indirect transaction taxes, refunds, reversals and chargebacks. Salaries, ordinary hosting, development and discretionary overhead are not deductions. Exactly 70% of nonnegative Net Subscription Revenue funds the community economy and 30% belongs to ADFi; the policy version and every source input are retained.

Only `QUALIFIED` ADFi-native events participate. Supported categories are qualified referrals, qualified downstream actions from referrals, accepted useful feedback on another member's work, approved collaboration contributions and, for Creators, qualified value from originated DISCOVER works. A click or YouTube-native engagement earns nothing. Qualification decisions expose reason codes and support moderation/appeal. Effective-dated accepted split versions apply at event time and never rewrite historic earnings. Wallets and payouts use immutable entries; failed payouts return money only through compensating entries.

Monthly close order is: lock period; reconcile receipts, fees, taxes, refunds and reversals; compute net revenue; allocate 70/30; evaluate qualified events under the period policy; allocate configurable sub-pools; apply effective split versions and rights reserves; create immutable member earnings; hold pending amounts; release eligible amounts to Available; process optional PayPal Payouts with idempotent batch/item IDs.
